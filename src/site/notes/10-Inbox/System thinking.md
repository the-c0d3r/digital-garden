# [[10-Inbox/System thinking\|System thinking]]
tags: #evergreen 


Instead of trying to solve the problem, think about how to build systems to solve it for you. This is also related to the [[01-Project/012-Efforts/ongoing/How to get rich\|How to get rich]], which talks about how getting skilled at a domain only increases your hourly rate, it doesn't generate wealth for you. 

Wealth is when you build systems (assets) that generate money for you without you working on it, or minimal working.
